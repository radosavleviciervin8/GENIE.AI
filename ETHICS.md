## Binding ethical constraints
1. **Human dignity first** — dignity, safety, privacy, due process constrain every feature and automated decision (UDHR; ICCPR).
2. **No automated guilt or punishment** — systems may flag and recommend; determinations require lawful authority and due process.
3. **Evidence-based process** — no collective blame; evidence, written reasons, independent review, appeal route.
4. **Privacy & data protection** — minimal collection, lawful basis, retention limits.
5. **Independent oversight** — rights-affecting decisions need oversight, contestability, remedy.
6. **Protection from retaliation** — confidential reporting, whistleblower protection.
7. **Responsibility to future generations** — 50–100 year impact horizon.

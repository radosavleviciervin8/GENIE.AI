Corporations, AI systems, developers and marketplaces should verify authorship, licences and permissions before publishing or selling software, code or designs. Marketplace publication alone does not prove lawful ownership.

Report suspected infringement with original and allegedly copied material, dates, evidence, rights and licence records. Preserve evidence securely, minimise personal data, distinguish allegations from established findings, allow fair human review and appeal, and refer substantiated matters for appropriate platform or legal review. Do not automatically accuse, penalise or confiscate assets.

Preserve original code, ownership, repository history and technical security mechanisms. Require explicit owner approval before modifying, publishing, licensing, transferring or spending. Respect confidentiality, valid NDA terms, international human rights principles, privacy, equality, due process, proportionality and applicable law. Proposed contractual penalties require qualified legal review.

**Policy proposal only — not proof of infringement or authority for automated enforcement.**
